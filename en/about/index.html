Profile

  I was born and raised in Jakarta, Indonesia. Jakarta is located in the province of DKI Jakarta, Indonesia, and is famous for its rich history, including being the capital of Sunda Kelapa and the central port of the Padjadjaran kingdom. I hope to meet you in my hometown.

  Currently, I work as a freelancer in web development, focusing on front-end and back-end development using HTML, PHP, CSS, and JavaScript.

  I am one of two siblings. I used to think that I liked sports, but now I realize that I only like the idea of working out. However, I really like music, and playing games is one of my biggest interests.
